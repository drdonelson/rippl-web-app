# Rippl Automotive — Proposed Rate Card
_For: DriveCentric dealers and multi-rooftop groups_

---

## Why Automotive Is Different

| Metric | Dental | Automotive |
|--------|--------|------------|
| Avg. deal gross profit | $1,500 LTV | $3,000–5,000 per deal |
| Vehicle sale price | — | $35,000+ |
| Referral close rate boost | 3–5× | 4–6× (friends trust friends on big purchases) |
| Reward that makes sense | $50–100 gift card | $100–250 gift card |
| Attribution complexity | Low | Medium (longer sales cycle) |

Automotive referrals are worth 2–3× a dental referral. Pricing should reflect that.

---

## Proposed Per-Referral Rate Card

| Tier | Rooftops | Per Rewarded Referral | Monthly Est. (8 refs/loc) |
|------|----------|-----------------------|--------------------------|
| Single Rooftop | 1 | **$75** | $600 |
| Group | 2–4 | **$65** | $1,040–$2,080 |
| Multi-Rooftop | 5+ | **$55** | $2,200–$5,500+ |

_Setup fee: $499 (waivable for DriveCentric launch partners)_

---

## What's Included

- Per-vehicle referral link in the DriveCentric CRM (or standalone QR code)
- New customer referral landing page (co-branded with dealership)
- Automated reward fulfillment on deal closed: Amazon gift card or in-house credit
- Showroom staff dashboard: referral pipeline, status, reward history
- Attribution: referral link → referral code → walk-in name match
- Automated customer SMS & email notifications

## Comparison vs. Lead Aggregators

| Source | Cost per Lead | Close Rate | Cost per Deal |
|--------|--------------|------------|--------------|
| Cars.com / AutoTrader | $50–150/lead | 5–10% | $500–3,000 |
| Google Ads | $30–80/click | 2–5% | $600–4,000 |
| **Rippl (referral)** | **$75/rewarded deal** | **~100%** | **$75** |

_Referrals are closed deals — you only pay when a deal is rewarded._

## DriveCentric Integration Note

Current status: no DC client yet. Integration approach options:

1. **Standalone**: Dealer uses Rippl dashboard independently; staff logs deals manually
2. **CRM embed**: Rippl referral link in DriveCentric customer card (requires DC webhook or API)
3. **SFTP sync**: Daily export from DriveCentric → Rippl (same pattern as Carlock SFTP)

Recommended path to first DC client: standalone launch, SFTP sync as V2.
