The customer experience — from purchase to payout

Zero friction
1

Customer buys a car at Volvo of Cool Springs

The deal is marked Delivered in DriveCentric as normal.

2

They're automatically enrolled in Carlock Rewards

Overnight, the system detects the delivery and sends a welcome text: "Congrats on your new car! Refer a friend and earn $100 when they buy."

3

They share their unique code or link

Every customer gets a personal Carlock Rewards code (e.g., SARAH4K2) — they share it by text, in conversation, or on their referral card.

4

The salesperson logs the referrer's code in the deal — a 10-second step that's already part of the delivery process.

5

The original customer gets a $100 reward — automatically

A text and email arrive: "Your friend just bought a car! Your $100 reward is ready." Redeemable as a Visa gift card, store credit, or charitable donation.
