1

Know when a referral is happening

A referral happens when a customer buys a vehicle because a friend, family member, or past customer sent them your way. Ask every customer at meet-and-greet — don't wait for them to bring it up.

The question to ask: "Did someone from our Carlock Rewards program send you in today?"
If yes — or if they mention a friend's name — that's a Carlock Rewards referral.
1

Buyer walks in (or calls)

Ask: "Did someone from our Carlock Rewards program refer you?" — even if they don't bring it up first.

2

They say yes

Note the referrer's name if you can get it. Then log it in DriveCentric when you create or update the deal.

3

One selection in DriveCentric — done

Select Type: Showroom and Source: Carlock Rewards. That's it. Everything else is automatic.

2

Make one selection on the deal

On the deal record, click the pencil icon next to the Source field (or set it when creating the deal). You'll see two dropdowns — set them exactly like this:

DriveCentric — Edit Source
Showroom ▾
Select "Showroom" from the Type dropdown
Carlock Rewards ▾
Then select "Carlock Rewards" from the Source dropdown
That's the entire process. Once the deal is marked Delivered, Carlock Rewards detects it overnight and the referrer gets their $100 reward automatically — no further action needed from you.
3

How we know who to reward

Carlock Rewards uses two paths to identify the referrer. You don't need to do anything differently — but knowing how it works helps if a customer asks.

Automatic

Referral Link

The referring customer shared their personal Carlock Rewards link with their friend before they visited. When the friend clicked it, we already captured the connection.

"My friend texted me her link and I clicked it last week."

No action needed — referrer is matched automatically when the deal closes.

Manual assist

Walk-In Name

The buyer walked in without clicking a link. Add a Customer Note on their profile with the referrer's name — our team will match it manually.

"My friend Sally Jones told me to come in."

Add a note: "Referred by Sally Jones" — our team handles the rest.

Either way: Select Showroom → Carlock Rewards on the deal. That's your job. Matching the referrer is ours.
4

What happens after the deal is delivered

You don't have to do anything else. Once the deal is marked Delivered in DriveCentric, Carlock Rewards takes over automatically.

✓

Overnight: Carlock Rewards scans delivered deals

Every morning, Carlock Rewards pulls the previous day's delivered deals and looks for "Carlock Rewards" source entries.

✓

New buyer is enrolled in Carlock Rewards

The new buyer automatically becomes a Carlock Rewards member — they'll receive a welcome text and their own referral link to share with friends.

✓

Referrer is notified and rewarded

The referring customer gets a text and email: "Great news — your friend just bought a car! Your $100 reward is ready." They claim it digitally — no action from your team.

5

Common questions

The buyer mentions a referrer but I already saved the deal with a different source. Can I fix it?
Yes — go back into the deal, click the pencil icon next to Source, and change it to Type: Showroom, Source: Carlock Rewards. Carlock Rewards processes deals every morning — as long as it's updated before the next sync, the referral will still be credited.
The buyer knows their friend referred them but didn't click any link. What do I do?
Select Showroom → Carlock Rewards on the deal, then add a Customer Note on their profile with the referrer's name (e.g., "Referred by Sally Jones"). Our team reviews these and manually matches the referral.
What if the buyer says "I found you on Google" but also mentions a friend?
Log it as Carlock Rewards. The referral takes priority — your customer's friend deserves their reward, and it's your team's relationship that brought them in.
What does the referrer actually receive?
A $100 Visa gift card delivered digitally by text and email — no physical card, no wait. They click the link, choose their reward, and it arrives instantly.
Does every customer who buys a car get enrolled automatically?
Yes — every delivered deal automatically enrolls the buyer in Carlock Rewards overnight. They'll get a welcome text with their own referral link so they can start referring friends right away.
