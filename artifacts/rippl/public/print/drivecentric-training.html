Staff Training Guide

Logging Referrals
in DriveCentric

How to make sure every referral gets credited — and your customers get their $100.

1

Know when a referral is happening

A referral happens when a customer buys a vehicle because a friend, family member, or past customer sent them your way. At some point during the deal — at the desk, on the phone, or when they first walk in — the buyer will mention who referred them.

Your job: Catch that moment. Ask every customer: "Did someone send you our way today?" If yes, note the referrer's name or Rippl code before moving on.
1

Buyer walks in (or calls)

Ask: "Did someone refer you to us?" — even if they don't bring it up first.

2

Get the referrer's name or code

Either their name ("John Smith sent me") or their Rippl code from their phone or referral card.

3

Log it in DriveCentric when creating the deal

Two fields to fill — takes 10 seconds. Shown on the next page.

2

Fill in two fields on the deal

On the deal record in DriveCentric, find the Source section. Fill in both fields below.

DriveCentric — Deal Source Section
Customer Referral
Always select "Customer Referral" from the dropdown
MIKEX7K2  —or—  John Smith
Enter the referrer's Rippl code OR their full name
That's the entire process. Once the deal is marked Delivered, Rippl automatically detects it overnight, matches the referrer, and sends them their $100 reward — no further action needed from you.
3

Code or name — either works

Preferred

Rippl Referral Code

Customer shows you their code from the Rippl text/app, or hands you their referral card.

MIKEX7K2

Type the code exactly as shown. Rippl matches it instantly — no ambiguity.

Fallback

Referrer's Full Name

Buyer says "Mike Johnson sent me" but doesn't have a code.

Mike Johnson

Type first and last name. Rippl will attempt to match it. Exact spelling helps.

Tip: Encourage referred customers to have their referral code ready before they arrive. It's in the text message they received when they enrolled in Rippl, or on their referral card.
4

What happens after the deal is delivered

You don't have to do anything else. Once the deal is marked Delivered in DriveCentric, Rippl takes over automatically.

✓

Overnight: Rippl scans delivered deals

Every morning, Rippl pulls the previous day's exported deals and looks for "Customer Referral" source group entries.

✓

Referrer is matched and notified

Rippl finds the referrer by their code or name and sends them a text and email: "Great news — your friend just bought a car! Your $100 reward is ready."

✓

Referrer claims their $100 Visa gift card

They click the link in the text, choose their reward, and it's delivered digitally. No action needed from your team.

5

Common questions

The buyer doesn't know their referrer's code or full name — just a first name. What do I enter?
Enter whatever you have (e.g., "Mike"). Our team will follow up to manually match the referral. It won't be lost — it goes to a review queue. Getting the full name or code is always better, so ask the buyer to check their texts from the person who referred them.
What if I forget to log it and the deal is already delivered?
Go back into the deal and update the Source Group and Source Description fields. Rippl processes the export daily — as long as the fields are filled in before the next morning's sync, the referral will still be credited.
What if the buyer says "I found you on Google" but then mentions a friend referred them too?
Log it as a referral. The referral source takes priority — your customer is owed their reward, and it's your team's relationship that made the sale happen.
Does the Source Group dropdown already have "Customer Referral" as an option?
This depends on your DriveCentric setup. If you don't see it, contact your DriveCentric administrator — they can add it. It only needs to be created once.
What does the referrer actually receive?
A $100 Visa gift card delivered by email and text — digitally, no physical card needed. They can use it anywhere Visa is accepted.
