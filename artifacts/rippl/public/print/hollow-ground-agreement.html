1. Purpose

This Agreement sets out the terms under which Partner is authorized to resell and white-label the Rippl referral rewards platform to salon clients under the Hollow Ground brand. Rippl provides the technology; Partner manages client relationships, pricing, and support.

2. White-Label License

Rippl grants Partner a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to:

  • Brand the Rippl platform as "Hollow Ground Powered by Rippl" in all client-facing materials.
  • Set Partner's own pricing, contracts, and service terms with end clients.
  • Resell access to Rippl's salon integration (Vagaro), automated referral detection, reward fulfillment, and SMS/email notification services.

Rippl retains all intellectual property in the platform. The "Powered by Rippl" attribution remains visible on the customer-facing reward page.

3. Fees Payable by Partner to Rippl

Partner pays Rippl a tiered platform fee plus a per-referral fee each month. Tango gift cards are always passed through at cost with no markup.

Active Client Offices Platform Fee / Office / Month Per Confirmed Referral Gift Cards (Tango)
1 – 5 $60 $12 At cost
6 – 15 $45 $12 At cost
16 + $30 $12 At cost

Example (4 clients, 6 referrals/client/month): Platform fee $240 + referral fee $288 + Tango gift cards at face value. Partner invoices clients separately at whatever price Partner chooses.

A "confirmed referral" is a referral event where the system records a successful referral match and a reward is delivered or an admin task is generated. Unmatched referrals that result in no reward are not charged.

4. Invoicing & Payment

  • Rippl invoices Partner by the 5th of each month for the prior month's activity.
  • Invoice line items: active office platform fees, confirmed referral count × $12, and Tango gift card costs with receipts attached.
  • Payment due Net 15 from invoice date.
  • Rippl maintains a funded Tango account to ensure immediate gift card delivery. Partner pre-funds or reimburses Tango costs as invoiced.
  • Rippl has no visibility into what Partner charges its clients. Partner's client pricing and billing are entirely independent.

5. Partner Responsibilities

  • Maintain active client relationships, onboarding, and first-level support for salon clients.
  • Provide Rippl with Vagaro credentials (Business ID, API key, API secret) for each new client prior to onboarding.
  • Ensure each client's salon staff understands and actively promotes the referral program.
  • Obtain any consent required under applicable laws (including TCPA) before Rippl sends SMS messages to client's customers.
  • Notify Rippl promptly if a client relationship terminates so the practice can be deactivated.

6. Rippl Responsibilities

  • Maintain platform uptime and Vagaro integration for all active client practices.
  • Fulfill gift card rewards via Tango automatically upon confirmed referral.
  • Provide Partner with a single-login dashboard to view all Hollow Ground client practices.
  • Make branding and reward configuration editable by Partner within the platform.
  • Provide reasonable support for technical issues within 2 business days.

7. Term & Termination

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues month-to-month. Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice. Upon termination:

  • All client practices will be deactivated after the 30-day notice period.
  • Partner remains responsible for all fees incurred through the termination date.
  • Rippl will provide a final invoice within 15 days of termination.

Either party may terminate immediately for material breach (e.g., non-payment after 30 days, misuse of the platform, or use that violates applicable law).

8. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep the terms of this Agreement and each other's business information confidential. Partner shall not disclose Rippl's platform pricing or architecture to any third party. Rippl shall not disclose Partner's client list or business terms.

9. Data & Privacy

Rippl processes customer data (name, phone, email) on behalf of each salon client. Rippl does not sell or share this data with third parties. Partner is responsible for ensuring each client's customer data is collected and processed lawfully, including any required consent for SMS communications.

10. Limitation of Liability

Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. Rippl's total liability under this Agreement is limited to fees paid by Partner in the prior 3 months.

11. Miscellaneous

  • This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Tennessee.
  • Any disputes will be resolved by good-faith negotiation first; if unresolved, by binding arbitration in Nashville, TN.
  • Amendments require written agreement by both parties.
  • This Agreement is the entire understanding between the parties on this subject and supersedes any prior discussions.
