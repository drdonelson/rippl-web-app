Revenue Share Options

Three models — pick the one that fits how you want to run the business

Model A

Pure Wholesale

You pay Rippl $50/office/mo
Gift cards At cost
Per-referral fee $0
You charge clients Your price
✓ Simple — flat, predictable
✓ Your margin scales with retail price
– David earns same regardless of volume
Model B

Per-Referral Only

Monthly platform fee $0
Gift cards At cost
You pay Rippl $15/referral
You charge clients Your price
✓ No cost when volume is low
✓ Pure value-aligned pricing
– Unpredictable for both parties
Recommended
Model C

Hybrid

You pay Rippl $35/office/mo
Gift cards At cost
Per-referral fee $12/referral
You charge clients ~$99/office/mo
✓ Predictable floor for Rippl
✓ Your margin: ~$64/office + upside
✓ Both sides win on volume
